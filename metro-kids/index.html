Metro Kids
Paperless

A centralized operations platform replacing disconnected spreadsheets, browser-storage workflows, and legacy Google Apps Script processes with a secure PostgreSQL-backed system.

The organization serves approximately 700 students; that figure describes organizational scale, not verified application users.

01 · The problem

From fragmented workflows to one dependable system.

Staff needed to manage child and guardian profiles, registrations, events, approvals, visitation, transportation, and reporting across disconnected tools. The goal was a system that made everyday operations easier while protecting sensitive records and preserving an audit trail.

02 · What I built

Operations

Profiles, registration and RSVP forms, event and program management, staff approvals, duplicate detection, record merging, and audit logs.

Transportation

Visitation planning, worker assignments, digital bus lists, route links, pickup sequencing, and scheduling.

Data workflows

CSV/XLS/XLSX imports and exports with validation, conflict detection, asynchronous processing, and reporting support.

Account safety

Staff authentication, four server-enforced roles, notifications, transactional email, password resets, and rate limiting.

04 · Reliability under concurrency

Unique IDs

PostgreSQL sequence-based student ID generation prevents duplicate identifiers during concurrent operations.

Route ordering

PostgreSQL advisory transaction locks protect transportation-route ordering when multiple updates compete.

Import safety

FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED lets asynchronous workers claim import jobs without processing the same job twice.

Idempotent workflows

Registration and RSVP flows validate, match duplicates, detect conflicts, and limit updates through approval-controlled paths.
